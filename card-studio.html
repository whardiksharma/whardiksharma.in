🧭 Epson L3251 Two-Pass Paper Flip & Reload Instructions (Manual Duplex Guide)
Top-Rear Feeder Guide
Step 1 • Print Fronts (P1)
Place blank A4 sheet in Epson top-rear tray. Click 📄 Print Fronts (P1). The page exits into the front tray with card fronts printed facing UP.
Top Tray ➔ [Blank A4] ➔ Prints Fronts (P1)
Step 2 • The Paper Flip (Turn Over)
Pick up printed sheet from front tray. Turn it over top-to-bottom (so the blank back is now facing UP / towards you). Keep the top edge at the top!
🔄 Turn Over: Top-to-Bottom (Blank Side UP)
Step 3 • Reload & Print Backs (P2)
Slide that top edge straight back into the top feeder. Click 📄 Print Backs (P2). Both sides will print right-side up, with exact cut alignment!
Top Tray ➔ [Blank Side Faces You] ➔ Print Backs (P2)
⚙️ Browser Print Dialog: Scale: 100% (Default) • Margins: None / Minimum • Background Graphics: ON
